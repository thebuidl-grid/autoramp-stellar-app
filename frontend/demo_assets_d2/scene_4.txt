Now I click Bridge. The wallet signs two transactions. First, it approves Circle's token messenger to spend the USDC. Second, it burns the USDC on Base, with Circle's forwarder contract set as the recipient on Stellar, and my Stellar account encoded in the message. As soon as the burn is confirmed, the source transaction hash appears in the status card, and AutoRamp starts tracking it. Next, Circle's attestation service signs the burn once the Base block has enough confirmations. Then a relayer submits the mint-and-forward call on Stellar, and the USDC is created directly in the destination account. This takes about two minutes, and everything you are seeing is live. Nothing is skipped or sped up while we wait.
