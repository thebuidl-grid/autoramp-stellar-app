Hello, this is a demonstration of Deliverable 2 of the AutoRamp Stellar Community Fund Instaward. The goal is to let users deposit USDC from a blockchain other than Stellar into AutoRamp's Stellar-based off-ramp. In this demo, the source network is Base, on its Sepolia testnet, and the destination network is Stellar testnet. Everything you will see on chain is a real testnet transaction, and every click and keystroke happens in the real application.
